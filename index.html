Distance Bands

Different journey lengths use different road characteristics. Short trips wind through lanes; long trips are mostly motorway. Each band has its own road factor (multiplier on straight-line distance) and average lorry speed.

Lorry Buffer (min)
Snap-down Threshold (min)

Buffer: mins added to car drive time (lorries are slower).
Snap-down: if travel falls within N mins of dropping to a cheaper trip band, snap down to grab the extra trip per day (e.g. 35min → 34min keeps 6 trips instead of 5).

Pipeline: straight-line distance → pick band → road = straight × factor → car min = road ÷ speed → + lorry buffer → snap-down if within threshold.
Use the "Travel Override" field on Search or Quote Builder to skip the auto-pipeline entirely and use a known exact time.
Round-trip time: 2× travel (collect+return) + 2× load/tip. Settings persist in browser local storage.
